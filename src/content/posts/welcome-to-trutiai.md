TrutiAI is a blog for people who build software and want writing that has actually been tried: tutorials where the code runs, comparisons where the tools were installed, and explainers that stop at the point where you can go and do the thing.

## What you will find here

- **Tutorials.** Step-by-step guides with the full code, the commands, and the mistakes we made along the way.
- **Tool comparisons.** Honest side-by-side tests of libraries, services and editors, with the free tiers and limits spelled out.
- **Explainers.** How something works under the hood, written so a developer with a different specialism can follow.
- **Building in public.** This site itself runs on Astro and Cloudflare's free plan; we will write up how it was built and what it costs.

## How we write

Every article is written by a person, from their own experience, and edited before publishing. Where AI tools helped with a draft or a diagram, the article says so. Corrections are made in place and dated. The full policy is on the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) page.

## Write with us

Community writing opens after the first few weeks. Writers sign in with Google, draft in a Medium-style editor, and every submission is checked before it goes live. The [community guidelines](/community-guidelines) explain what is welcome.

Questions or ideas? Use the [contact page](/contact). New articles arrive several times a week; the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) is the simplest way to follow along.
