## The short version

Sentry's free Developer plan covers one user and 5,000 errors a month, which is enough for a small site. The alternatives trade features for self-hosting.

## Sentry

Hosted, generous free tier, excellent source-map support.

## Bugsnag

Hosted, smaller free tier, strong mobile support.

## GlitchTip

Self-hosted, Sentry-compatible SDKs, no per-seat pricing.
